## What the empirical sources actually show
The 2025 Communications Biology study examined 20 healthy participants selected from 561 retreat participants before and after a seven-day intervention combining meditation, reconceptualization, and open-label placebo healing rituals. It reported changes in fMRI connectivity and multiple plasma-based molecular measures. The paper explicitly describes the work as observational and discusses limitations and confounds.

The 2025 twin pilot involved six twin sets (12 participants) at a seven-day Dispenza-led retreat and collected multimodal data including qEEG, wearable data, and blood samples. It is a small pilot and requires replication.

The 2023 complexity-matching paper examined HRV dynamics in eight healers and one healee during a heart-focused meditation protocol and used nonlinear mathematical methods to quantify changes in complexity matching.

The 2026 RNG paper examined 15 group healing meditations involving large participant groups and reported a significant correlation between local and global RNG network coherence. It is explicitly exploratory and does not establish a causal mechanism by which consciousness alters distant RNGs.

## Engineering extraction
1. intentional attention can be represented as a controllable variable;
2. repeated practice can change persistent state;
3. reconceptualization can be represented as an explicit self-model update;
4. affective/valence variables can influence the trajectory field;
5. coherence can be treated as a measurable consistency variable;
6. group experiments can test whether multiple agents produce detectable synchronization above null models.

## Implementation candidate
ATTENTION + INTENTION + VALENCE + SELF-MODEL UPDATE -> STATE TRANSFORMATION -> COHERENCE -> NEW REGIME

A separate experimental layer can implement multi-agent synchronization experiments without assuming a nonlocal consciousness mechanism.

## Experimental questions
- Does intentional self-model rehearsal change later trajectory selection more than neutral rehearsal?
- Does repeated coherence-oriented practice improve longitudinal state stability?
- Can group-agent synchronization exceed independently specified null models?
- Are observed effects explained by ordinary communication, shared inputs, timing, expectation, or common environmental variables?

## Important boundary
The published Dispenza-associated studies provide empirical data about meditation retreats and associated biological or signal-level measures. They do not establish the stronger metaphysical claims sometimes associated with Dispenza's broader teaching system.
