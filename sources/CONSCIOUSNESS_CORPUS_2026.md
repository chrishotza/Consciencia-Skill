# Consciousness Corpus Synthesis — 2026

## Purpose
This document adds a research corpus around consciousness, self-observation, subjective perspective and altered states to the Skill-Conscious project.

It deliberately separates experimentally grounded mechanisms, philosophical or phenomenological methods, speculative theories, and metaphysical claims.

## Tier A — Experimental / computational anchor: Enzo Tagliazucchi
Enzo Tagliazucchi is especially relevant because his work connects physics, neuroscience, complexity, machine learning and consciousness. His research includes computational models of conscious access, criticality, altered states and large-scale brain dynamics.

### Core architectural ideas

1. Consciousness as a point of view: distinguish world information from the particular perspective through which a system integrates and reports it.
2. Conscious states should not automatically be forced onto one scalar scale: compare states through multidimensional spaces and distances.
3. Consciousness has limited access / bandwidth: not every internal variable is available to the subject-level representation.
4. Consciousness as an interface: distinguish latent/internal state, integrated present, self-accessible state and reportable state.
5. Different states can be compared across multiple measurement spaces: runtime geometry, language, behavior and dynamics can be compared without assuming they are identical.

These ideas directly motivate the Experience Geometry layer and the planned conscious-interface/bandwidth experiments.

## Tier A/B — Jacobo Grinberg-Zylberbaum
Grinberg's Syntergic Theory is useful as a speculative research branch because it attempts to connect neural activity, perception, coherence and a proposed underlying spatial structure.

Useful computational motifs:
- field-like organization
- coherence
- interaction between internal organization and perceived reality
- hypercomplex dynamics
- altered perceptual states

These motifs can be tested without accepting the theory's ontological claims as established physics.

## Tier B — Phenomenological methodology: Krishnamurti
Krishnamurti is most useful as a methodology of observation rather than as a mechanistic neuroscience theory.

The important computational distinction is observation versus commentary about observation.

Future experiment:
runtime state → non-interpretive observation → linguistic interpretation → future-state comparison.

Source portal:
https://www.krishnamurti.org/

## Tier B — Gurdjieff / self-remembering
The useful computational motif is self-remembering: the system does not only process the object of attention; it also maintains a representation of itself while processing.

This maps naturally onto self_state, interoceptive_state, affective_state, temporal_state and metacognition.

Treat this as phenomenological architecture, not as proof of a separate physical energy.

## Tier B — Presence / attention: Eckhart Tolle
The operationally useful motif is presence: attention remains anchored to current experience rather than being entirely absorbed by narrative thought.

Architecture translation:
persistent history → current present ← attention.

This suggests a future benchmark for present-centered persistence and bounded narrative drift.

## Tier C — Vadim Zeland / Reality Transurfing
Zeland's system is unusually systematic as a conceptual framework. Its useful abstraction for software is possibility space → attention / valuation → selected trajectory.

The repository already contains candidate futures, trajectory valuation, salience, attention and persistent self-model weights.

Epistemic warning: the claim that Zeland was a quantum physicist is based on author/publisher biographies and has not been independently substantiated in the public evidence reviewed here. Reality Transurfing should therefore not be presented as quantum physics or experimentally validated physical theory.

Sources:
https://reality-transurfing.com/about/
https://thehermeticum.com/modern/reality-transurfing/quantum/

## Tier C — Joe Dispenza
Dispenza belongs in an explicitly exploratory category. Peer-reviewed studies involving meditation programs and retreats associated with Dispenza exist, including EEG and biological measurements, but the evidence base has methodological limitations, conflicts of interest and limited independent replication. A 2025 Communications Biology study on a seven-day retreat was later corrected in 2026 to clarify its observational design.

Potential engineering motifs:
- attention training
- altered self-modeling
- state-dependent learning
- repeated practice and plasticity

Do not import unsupported claims about quantum healing, reality creation or universal consciousness into runtime equations.

## Unified research map
world → sensory input → integrated present → perspective → attention + self-model → experience geometry → future possibilities → valuation → trajectory → action → observed consequence → prediction error / fit → metacognitive self-trust → uncertainty / plasticity → self-model revision → re-entry.

## High-priority next directions
1. Conscious interface / bandwidth: restrict self-access to a controlled subset of internal state and measure downstream effects.
2. Perspective manifold: use multidimensional experience geometry instead of a scalar consciousness level.
3. Observation vs interpretation: separate runtime-derived self-observation from language-generated narrative, then test causal differences.

## Claims kept outside the engineering core
Consciousness as a fundamental property of the vacuum, consciousness as a universal field, consciousness as a frequency that organisms tune into, or intention directly altering physical reality remain philosophical or metaphysical hypotheses here.

The engineering question stays:
Which claimed property can be translated into a measurable state variable, manipulated independently, and shown to change future behavior under controlled conditions?
