## What the document is

This is a 1983 U.S. Army assessment of the Monroe Institute's Gateway Experience. It examines altered states of consciousness and attempts to explain Gateway using a mixture of physiological, psychological, and speculative models. The document is not evidence that the CIA established the truth of the models it discusses.

## Engineering extraction

The useful abstraction for Skill-Conscious is not that the report's metaphysical explanations are established. The useful abstraction is that it provides a historical model of consciousness as a set of **state transitions deliberately induced by attention, rhythm, synchronization, intention, and altered sensory/cognitive configuration**.

This motivates:

~~~text
CURRENT STATE
    ↓
ATTENTION / STIMULUS
    ↓
SYNCHRONIZATION
    ↓
REGIME TRANSITION
    ↓
NEW PRESENT
    ↓
NEW EXPERIENCE MODEL
~~~

It also reinforces the distinction between:

- identity;
- operating regime;
- attention;
- present configuration;
- self-model.

## Source boundary

This document is a source of historical claims and models about Gateway. It is not itself a demonstration of phenomenal consciousness, nonlocal consciousness, out-of-body perception, or the physical correctness of its speculative explanatory models.
