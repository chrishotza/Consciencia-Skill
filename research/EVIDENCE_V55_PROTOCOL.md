# V55 — Evidence Protocol

**Status: protocol implemented; empirical result pending.**

Required controls:

1. matched warmup trajectories;
2. perturbations of both signs;
3. memory and self-model held fixed at perturbation time;
4. selection-enabled and selection-disabled arms;
5. exact recovery horizon;
6. raw trajectory artifacts retained.

Evidence boundary:

V55 tests resilience and identity preservation under a controlled state
perturbation. It is an operational criterion derived from the project's ontology,
not a demonstration of phenomenological consciousness.
